# Jeans & Denim — Category Notes

Denim has the highest size-related return rate of any category in the catalog, driven by
inconsistent sizing across brands and styles (skinny, straight, bootcut) rather than
customer behavior. A customer returning multiple pairs of jeans in different sizes from
the same brand, in the same order, should be treated as a fit-finding pattern, not a risk
signal.

Repeat purchases of the exact same SKU in the same size after a prior return of that SKU
is a stronger signal worth surfacing — it suggests either a sizing/manufacturing defect in
that specific product (a catalog-quality issue) or, less commonly, pattern abuse.

Denim is low value-per-unit relative to outerwear, so the cost of a false-positive risk
flag (frustrating a normal size-seeking customer) generally outweighs the cost of a missed
low-value fraud case in this category.
