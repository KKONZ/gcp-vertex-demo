# General Returns Policy

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unworn, unwashed, and with original
tags attached. Final sale items (marked at 70% off or greater) are not eligible for
return. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the
returned item passing inspection.

Items showing wear, odor, alteration, or damage not present at time of shipment may be
rejected at inspection and returned to the customer rather than refunded.

A customer's overall return rate is not, on its own, a basis for denying an individual
return — but it is a relevant signal when combined with other risk indicators (see
`fraud-indicators.md`) for prioritizing manual review versus automatic approval.
