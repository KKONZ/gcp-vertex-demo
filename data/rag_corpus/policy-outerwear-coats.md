# Outerwear & Coats — Category Notes

Outerwear and coats carry a higher average order value than most categories and a longer
consideration cycle — customers frequently order two sizes or two colors of the same coat
to compare in person and return one. This is expected behavior, not a risk signal by
itself: a single-item return alongside a kept companion item from the same order is the
single most common, lowest-risk return pattern in this category.

Seasonal fit variance is real: coats bought for layering over sweaters in colder months
are sometimes returned as "too small" when ordered for warmer-weather wear instead.
Sizing-related return reasons in this category should be weighted less heavily toward
risk than "changed my mind" or undisclosed reasons.

Higher price point means higher fraud exposure per incident — wardrobing (wearing an item
once, e.g. for an event, then returning it) is disproportionately common here relative to
lower-priced categories.
